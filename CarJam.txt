Car Jam — straight lot and vehicle footprints
Tap a vehicle with a clear route in its arrow direction. Compact cars hold 2 passengers, sedans 4, minibuses 6 and large buses 10. Vehicles occupy 1–4 spaces and have distinct bodies. They drive out, use five pickup bays and board matching passengers. Full vehicles depart. Undo and Hint remain available. An existing save keeps its level and starts a fresh lot under the new footprint rules.
