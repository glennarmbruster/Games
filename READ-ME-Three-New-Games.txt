POCKET CLUB — 8-BALL POOL
Computer: Easy / Medium; pass-and-play; solo practice.
Tap or drag on the table to aim; arrows fine-tune the angle. Set power and Shoot.
Pocket your solids or stripes, then the 8. A foul gives the opponent ball in hand:
tap a clear spot to place the cue ball. Early 8 or scratch with the 8 loses.
Friendly house rules: no called pockets, groups can be assigned on the break,
and an 8 on the break is re-spotted. Practice lets you play both sides.
Menu pauses moving balls. A rack resumes after leaving/reloading.

METRO LINES — SUBWAY ROUTE PLANNER
Growing City challenge / Relaxed mode without crowding losses.
Select a colored route, then tap stations in order (or drag the first connection).
Passengers want stations matching their tiny shape. Trains run automatically;
shared stations let riders transfer between routes. Extend routes to new stops.
More than eight waiting riders starts a 20-second crowding ring in Growing City.
Every 45 seconds choose more seats, a route (maximum five), or an extra train
(maximum three on the selected route). Pause to edit; Undo stop removes the last
station. Extra trains are retained when rebuilding a route.
The city saves automatically. Each new city has a new station sequence.

REVERSI — GAME CHEST
Computer: Easy / Medium / Hard; pass-and-play.
Place on a dotted square to trap opposing discs in a straight line between your
discs. Trapped discs flip. Black goes first; no legal move automatically passes.
When neither player can move, the most discs wins. Corners are valuable.
Undo returns to your previous turn; Hint suggests a legal move.
Game Chest returns to all four modes; Exit returns to the main collection.
The three original Game Chest games and their saves are preserved.
