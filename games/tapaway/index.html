How to play

  1. Drag to turn the shape any way you like. Pinch to zoom.
  2. Tap a line and it slides out head first, the way its arrow points, and flies off over the edge.
  3. If another line is anywhere on its way out, it bumps into it and goes back. That's a mistake, and it still uses a move.
  4. Clear every line before you run out of moves. Stars: no mistakes = 3, one or two = 2, more = 1.
  5. HARD levels have fewer spare moves.
  6. Locked lines (a colored line with a lock) can only leave after the key line of the same color has gone.
  7. Timer lines (a red number) must be cleared before the count reaches 0. Every tap counts it down.
  8. Hint shows a line that can leave. You earn coins every level; coins buy more hints.

  2. Tap a block and it flies off the way its arrow points, if nothing is in the way. A dot shows where an arrow points straight at you.
  3. If another block is in the way, it bumps into it and goes back.
  4. Clear every block to win the level.
  5. Gold blocks pay 5 coins when they fly off. Every level pays coins too.
  6. Challenges (from level 10) have a limited number of moves; every tap counts, bumps too.
  7. Bomb: tap it, then a block: that block and everything touching it (a 3×3×3 space) is gone, without using a move. Coins buy more bombs and new skins.
