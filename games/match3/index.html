How to play

Garden Journey: match pieces to earn Bloom. Prune costs 12, Rain clears a row for 24, and Mix costs 18. These abilities cost no moves or coins. Three stars: finish with 15% of moves left and no inventory boosters or continues. Earned Bloom is always allowed. The orb goal means create power-ups. Prune clears one tile or damages one obstacle layer; Rain clears a row or damages its obstacles; Mix rearranges movable pieces. Earn one Bloom per matched ordinary piece and six per special-piece fusion, up to 24.

  1. Swipe a piece into its neighbor (or tap one, then the other) to line up 3 or more of a kind.
  2. Finish the level’s goals before the moves run out.
  3. Bigger matches make power-ups. 4 in a line: a Rocket (it points across your match). 2×2: a Propeller. L or T: TNT. 5 in a line: a Light Ball.
  4. Tap a power-up to set it off, or swipe it. Swipe two power-ups together for a combo. Swipe a Light Ball into a piece to clear every piece of that kind.
  5. Boxes and stones break when you match next to them; grass clears when you match on it; ice breaks when the piece inside is matched; acorns are collected at the bottom.
  6. Boosters don’t use a move: Hammer (one tile), Arrow (a row), Cannon (a column), Shuffle. Before a level you can start with a Rocket, TNT or Light Ball. Coins buy more.
  7. In Classic, every win gives one star and coins. Every 5th level is hard; every 10th is super hard. Garden Journey has 24 named gardens with up to three stars each.
