How to play

  1. Shake the 16 letter dice, then find as many words as you can in 3 minutes.
  2. Drag your finger from letter to letter. Each letter must touch the one before it (sideways, up, down or corner to corner), and you can't use the same die twice in a word. Lift your finger to try the word.
  3. Words need 3 or more letters. Qu is one die and counts as two letters.
  4. Points: 3 or 4 letters = 1, 5 = 2, 6 = 3, 7 = 5, 8 or more = 11.
  5. 2 players: take turns on the same board, 3 minutes each. When both of you find a word, it's crossed out and scores nothing.
  6. When time's up you'll see every word that was on the board.
