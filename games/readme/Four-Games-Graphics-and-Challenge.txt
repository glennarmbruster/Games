Goobs Games — Crowd Cascade, Lot Escape, Traffic Jam and Marble Grove

Installation
Upload this ZIP's contents to the collection root with folder paths preserved.
There are 11 files. No rollback ZIP or other game's update is included.
The service worker cache versions have been updated for these games and the
older Squad Sprint link, which opens the same Crowd Cascade game.

Crowd Cascade
- Rounded, articulated runners with helmets, visors, armor, backpacks and boots.
- Brighter coastal bridge, detailed rails and lamps, islands and water ripples,
  more detailed weapon crates and truck wheels, and enemy health bars.
- Red gates subtract runners. Shooting recruit gates improves the displayed
  reward by up to 14; a red gate that becomes positive turns blue.
- Striped barricades block shots and cost runners on contact. Dodge them.
- Faster courses, tougher squads, and an unavoidable final boss. The gap between
  the final guards gives a clear central firing lane toward the boss.
- Shot spread and weapon-crate health tuned so upgrades can be earned.
- Course number and earned stars preserved. One game, one mode.

Lot Escape (Classic)
- Bigger grids earlier, filled more densely with smaller cars.
- Level 18: 10 x 11 grid with 39 cars, previously 8 x 8 with 21 cars.
- Later grids grow to 12 x 16. All generated lots still have an exit sequence.
- Harbor Escape challenge mode and its rules are unchanged.
- A partly played current lot is retained. Restart it or finish it to see the
  new density. An untouched old lot with outdated dimensions is regenerated.

Traffic Jam
- Busy building artwork replaced with quiet grass parks, a few trees, benches
  and simple paths. Detailed cars and asphalt remain.
- Level 7: 22 vehicles instead of 14, with a longer blocking dependency sequence.
- Routes still guarantee a solution. Simultaneous departures, route clearance,
  Hint and Undo are retained.
- A partly cleared saved level keeps its old layout. Restart or finish it to use
  the new generation. Untouched old boards upgrade immediately.

Marble Grove
- Six rotating path families: oval spiral, rounded terrace, crescent, clover,
  riverbend and diamond. Portrait and landscape retain the same family.
- Faster chains: level 1 speed 22 versus about 16 before; level 4 speed 33.4
  versus 17.6 before. The progression caps at 70.
- Four colors from level 3 and five from level 5; longer chains and fewer
  ready-made pairs make aiming and color swapping more important.
- Added marble highlights and contact shadows, a more detailed glowing portal,
  and visible guardian recoil.
- Existing saved runs keep their route until completion or restart. New runs
  use the new path families. Levels, scores and progress remain intact.

- Lot Escape: 40 consecutive levels plus sampled levels through 1000 solved;
  board occupancy checked; both modes rendered at phone/tablet sizes.
