Neon Bricks — Endless and Patterns
Endless keeps the descending-wall game. Patterns incorporates Prism Breaker. Each retains its own save key.
Start with at least 60 balls; bricks have higher counts and difficulty rises. Every fifth destroyed brick releases a power-up: extra balls, Laser, Blast or a doubled next volley. Laser removes the lowest row. Blast halves brick counts. Recall returns an unfinished volley. Mode selector opens both games from one icon.
