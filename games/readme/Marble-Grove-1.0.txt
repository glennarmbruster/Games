MARBLE GROVE 1.0 — Goobs Games

Install: upload all files in this ZIP, preserving their folder paths. This adds
Marble Grove to the Arcade tab and its assets to offline caching. Accept the
collection's update prompt, reload, and open the game online before testing in
airplane mode. Apply this after the previously supplied collection updates.

PLAY
Tap a marble to shoot, or drag to aim and release. Match three or more of the
same color. Tap the guardian or Swap to exchange the current and next colors.
Matching groups pull together after clears and can produce combo multipliers.
Slow time lasts eight seconds, once per level. Clear the entire chain before
its leading marble reaches the hole. The counter includes marbles entering
the track. There are no ads, purchases, or network dependencies during play.

Levels continue offline using seeded marble arrangements, four spiral
orientations, varying curves, longer chains and up to five colors. Color and
pattern both identify marble types. The board adapts to portrait and landscape.
Current chain, level, score and best score save locally. A missed shot has no
separate life penalty; the moving chain provides the time pressure.

VALIDATION
Checked matches, nonmatching insertion, cascading combos, win/loss, rotation,
slow time, save restoration and full level completion through simulated real
tap/release shots. Checked Canvas renders at 320x568, 390x844, 844x390, 768x1024
and 1024x768. Actual Safari touch play, audio and installed offline behavior
still need device testing. No other gameplay files are changed.

ARTWORK
Built-in image generation produced grove.webp and marblegrove-v1.webp.
Marbles, the guardian and the track are drawn by the game for sharp resizing.
