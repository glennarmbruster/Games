GOOBS GAMES — COLLECTION REVIEW (RECOMMENDATIONS ONLY)
October 9, 2026

SCOPE AND LIMITS
All 65 game icons were opened and sampled at 390x740 portrait and 844x390 landscape. Opening screens, sampled game boards, visible navigation and runtime page errors were checked. No JavaScript page errors were recorded in this sweep. Selected pause routes were additionally probed. This is not a full playthrough of every mode/level, a native iOS test, or an offline persistence certification. Some screenshots caught opening transitions; transient transparency was not counted as a defect. Hidden Exit during active play is intentional for the approved pause-only games.

Only Moonshot gameplay was changed. Other findings below are recommendations, not fixes. Prior claims that all navigation was fully consistent were too broad: the sweep still shows legacy bottom bars, a few overlaps, and opening-screen navigation gaps.

MOONSHOT CHANGE
Exit velocity increased by exactly 8%: base 50 -> 54; each Power level 0.75 -> 0.81. Launch angle, timing/spray, scoring, fences and upgrade limits unchanged. Controlled identical-swing checks at Power 0, 10, 20, 45 confirmed the multiplier and unchanged angle/spray. At Power 10, the sample flight increased from 510 to 560 feet. Results vary with swing and pitch.
Upgrade availability: Power 45 + Contact 45 + Batting Eye 45 = 135 total upgrade levels. Each player level-up gives three spendable points. This is the game's capacity; your device's owned/unspent count was not read. The recovery screen's lower limits apply to its restoration workflow, not normal earning.

POTENTIAL ADDITIONS — ONLY FOUR RECOMMENDED
1. Pool (8-ball / later 9-ball): strongest missing sports/table game. Aim the cue, set power and pocket your group; finish with the 8-ball. Suggested offline AI and pass-and-play.
Reference: https://miniclip.com/games/8-ball-pool
2. Subway route planner: a distinct planning game. Connect stations with lines and manage limited trains as passenger demand grows. An original game in this genre would add more variety than another matching puzzle.
Reference: https://dinopoloclub.com/games/mini-metro/
3. Reversi: place a disc to sandwich the opponent's discs and flip them. Most discs at the end wins. Simple to learn; a natural fourth Game Chest mode.
Rules: https://www.worldothello.org/about/about-othello/othello-rules/official-rules/english
4. Mexican Train dominoes: match numbered ends on your train or eligible shared trains; shed tiles and avoid leftover points. Suggested legal-move highlights and automatic doubles handling.
Rules: https://www.pagat.com/domino/star/mextrain.html
Backgammon is optional, not a necessary gap. It combines dice-based movement, racing pieces home and sending exposed opponents back to restart. I would prioritize the four above unless that sounds appealing.
