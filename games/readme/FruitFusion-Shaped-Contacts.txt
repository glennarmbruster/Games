FRUIT FUSION — GRAPE / PEAR CONTACT FIX — v1.7.1

Upload the three included files to their matching paths.

Grapes and pears previously used one round collision circle each. Empty corners
around a tapered grape bunch could act as solid support, leaving visible air
between fruit that the physics considered touching.

These two fruits now use compound contact discs fitted inside their visible
flesh. Decorative leaves and transparent lower corners do not support the pile.
Bag floor/wall contacts use these same fitted shapes.

Artwork, fruit sizes (including strawberry and jackfruit), score rules and the
existing forgiving matching-fruit merge distance are unchanged. Saved runs,
Continue, and portrait/landscape restoration are retained. An existing saved
pile settles using the updated contact shapes when play resumes.

Validation: a false grape-corner contact reproduced and rejected by the new
shape; floor settling for grapes and pears; an approximation of the reported
pile; all 11 merge transitions; unchanged strawberry/jackfruit artwork; Continue,
Exit/reload and repeated portrait/landscape tests. Physical Safari unavailable.
