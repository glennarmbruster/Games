Fruit Fusion — gentle physics update

Upload these files to the collection root, preserving their folder paths.
This ZIP contains only Fruit Fusion's game.js, the cache version update in
sw.js, and this note. No other game code is included.

Changes
- Fruit can move sideways when struck. A small rebound applies to harder
  impacts; resting contacts stay stable. Floor friction now depends on time,
  rather than being repeatedly multiplied by the collision solver.
- Pineapple, grapes and watermelon: physical radius and artwork reduced 12%.
- Pear: reduced 10%. Apple: reduced 7%.
- Sprite source bounds include the full grape stem/leaves and pineapple crown.
  The pear crop also excludes stray pineapple leaf tips from the adjacent cell.
- Matching now allows a small 8% contact tolerance to account for artwork
  extending beyond the collision circles. An intervening fruit blocks this
  allowance; the final jackfruit still does not merge.
- Bag size, controls, scores and the merge sequence are unchanged.
  Two pineapples still merge into one jackfruit.

Validation
- Simulated an off-center drop that pushes two previously separated matching
  fruits together, then checked that the resulting pile settles.
- Checked collision behavior at 30, 60 and 120 updates per second.
- Checked the exact size factors, pineapple-to-jackfruit merge, overflow ending
  and portrait/landscape canvas renders at phone and tablet dimensions.
- Checked close-contact merging for every fruit rank, separation limits,
  intervening-fruit blocking and the non-merging final jackfruit.
- Syntax and ZIP integrity checked. Physical iPhone/iPad testing is still needed.

Suggested first test
Drop fruit slightly to one side of a resting piece to push it toward a matching
neighbor. The effect is intentionally gentle; larger fruit is harder to move.
