CAR JAM 2.1 — VISUAL REFINEMENT

Install over the Car Jam 2.0 update. Extract into the main Goobs-Games directory, preserving folders and replacing the included files. sw.js belongs in the main directory. How to now shows “Car Jam 2.1 · Bright town”.

Changes:
- Dark gray asphalt with subtle grain in WebGL.
- Brighter matching colors and less washed-out lighting.
- Reshaped compact cars and sedans, sloping cabin glass, hoods, mirrors, grilles, lights, tire/hub details and better bus trim.
- Passengers now have hair, varied skin tones, faces, jackets, trousers and shoes.
- Richer building colors, cornices, brick details, shop signs, doors, window boxes and striped awnings.
- Pickup bays moved away from the jam, leaving a clear drive lane in portrait. Landscape bays and approach aisles are also farther from the lot.
- Departure animation uses an aisle rather than driving straight across occupied pickup bays.

The matching rules, generated lots, vehicle capacities, Hint, Undo and saved progress remain unchanged. Other games are untouched.

Verification: local JavaScript and software-rendered scene checks covered five phone/tablet viewport sizes, selection, boarding, Undo, hints, full-lot completion, next level, rotation and saved-state restoration. Final layout checks verified bay boundaries and boarding in small portrait and landscape views. Actual Safari/WebGL appearance and device performance still need your device test.
