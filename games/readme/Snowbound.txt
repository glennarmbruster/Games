Snowbound 0.10.0 — early gameplay and visual revision

Install this ZIP at the Goobs Games root, preserving folders. Existing saved scores and upgrades carry forward.

The floating diamonds are gone. Stars grant a rocket refill, a timed shield, or a speed surge. The sled has a new hull and Rocket level 3 unlocks visible dual thrusters with stronger boost. New snow-laden pines, colorful lodges, course flags and brighter scenery accompany a slightly faster ride. The menu icon is new illustrated artwork.

This is still an early playable version, not a finished 1.0.
