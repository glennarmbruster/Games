Snowbound 0.2.0 — substantial first rebuild

Install the ZIP at the Goobs Games root, preserving its folders. Existing saved best distances and upgrades carry forward. The shared 3D engine is already present in the collection and cached for offline use.

Changes: chase camera, rolling 3D mountain, forest, launch gate and pullback, ramps, jumping, rocket boost, visible wing and rocket hardware, five saved upgrade paths. Both Launch & Glide and Endless Ride remain.

This is an early playable revision, not a finished 1.0.
