SNOWBOUND — Goobs Games

Copy this ZIP's files into the main Goobs Games directory, preserving paths.
Open Arcade > Snowbound. The game works offline once the collection update has finished installing.

Launch & Glide: choose power or pull down and release on the opening screen. Drag left/right to steer. Tap Jump to clear low obstacles. After each run choose one free upgrade: Launch, Glide or Steering. Upgrades and best distance save on this device.

Endless Ride: steer around obstacles, jump over low rocks and logs, use ramps, and try to beat your best distance. You have three chances per run. No tickets or wait time.

Keyboard: Left/Right or A/D to steer, Space to jump, Escape to pause.
Pause, Sound, and Exit are always in the top row while riding.
