SNOWBOUND 1.0 ALPHA 1 — mountain-flight rebuild

Install: upload this ZIP's contents at the Goobs Games root, preserving folders. This updates Snowbound and its cache version. The main collection menu and approved Snowbound icon are unchanged.

Rebuilt terrain and movement based on the three supplied Sled Surfers videos: wide banked ice, steep rolling hills, momentum-driven automatic takeoffs, sustained airborne glides, higher chase camera, elastic launch, star trails, snow forests, ice gardens, castle sections and finish arches. Original character and scenery assets are retained/refined rather than copying the reference game's assets.

Drag down and release to launch (or set power and tap Launch). Drag left/right to steer. Rockets add momentum; wings improve flight. Flip is enabled when airborne: finish the rotation before landing. Stars and air time earn additional permanent upgrade tokens. Open Garage from the opening menu or upgrade after a run. Dual thrusters remain available at Rocket level 3.

Both existing modes remain. Launch runs end when momentum runs out or at the course finish. Endless mode maintains a minimum speed and allows three rock impacts. Snowbanks and rocks cost momentum. The two existing cumulative level goals remain 7,500 m and 10,000 m. Complete runs to record progress; leaving an unfinished run does not bank its distance. Existing best distances, upgrade levels, earned upgrade tokens and level progress are preserved.

Fullscreen with a compact overlay HUD. Pause reveals Exit immediately, plus How to play. Rotation resets the camera projection without resetting the run. Icon unchanged.

Verification: scripted execution of the actual game logic and Three.js scene with a stub renderer, checking save migration, level overflow, launches, finite geometry/physics, steering, pause, rotation, collisions, rockets, flips and both modes. No browser GPU rendering or physical iPhone/iPad testing was available. This is an alpha for your playtest, not a claim of visual or gameplay parity with the reference.

Alpha 1 refinement: smoother frame-rate-independent camera/FOV changes, camera clearance over terrain, landing compression and snow bursts, airborne sled pitch, ground shadow, snowfall, crystal arches and scenery section notices. Garage upgrades now explain their benefit. Stationary scenery is combined by material to reduce rendering submissions while keeping animated pickups and turbines separate. Underlying momentum tuning is unchanged from the approved first rebuild.
