Snowbound 0.9 Alpha — two level preview

Install this ZIP at the Goobs Games root, preserving folders. It keeps the winning Snowbound icon from 0.10 and carries over saved best distances, upgrades and settings.

Level 1: Alpine Village. Accumulate 7,500 meters across runs in either mode to unlock Level 2.
Level 2: Aurora Pass. Accumulate another 10,000 meters across runs to clear the two level preview. Extra distance in the clearing run counts toward Level 2. The second course has a cooler blue and violet setting, more ice scenery and a different obstacle pattern. After both targets are met, Aurora Pass stays playable; more settings can follow after the screenshot review.

This pass also raises the chase camera modestly, refines the snowy tree boughs, and adds brighter alpine color. The icon and steering stay as in 0.10.
