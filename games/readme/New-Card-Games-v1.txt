WHAT IS INCLUDED
Solitaire Associations: a separate word-card solitaire game with Relaxed, Challenge and Daily Deal. Category crowns, hidden cards, stock/waste, hints, undo and saved progress.
Rummy: two-player straight Rummy against the computer, melds, layoffs and match scoring.
Gin Rummy: opening upcard offers, automatic meld optimization, knocking, gin, layoffs and undercuts.
Pinochle: single-deck partnership game with three computer players, bidding, trump, melds, legal trick play and contract scoring.
All four include detailed in-game instructions and controls. Rules and house variations are stated in each guide.
