JUICE SLIDE — LAUNCH-LINE MERGE FIX — v1.7.1

Upload these three files to matching paths in the existing collection.

Fixed matching drinks bouncing apart near the starting line. The old code
blocked merging until both drinks were more than 0.12 seconds old, while still
applying normal collision bounce. A newly launched beer could therefore bounce
away before it became eligible to merge.

Matching drinks now merge immediately on contact, including new launches and
newly formed drinks in chain matches. Applies to all 12 drink types.
Nonmatching collisions, scoring values, graphics, settings and saved progress
are unchanged.

Validation: reproduced the old beer-on-beer failure; verified 32 near-launch
contacts at 20, 30, 60 and 120 fps, head-on and offset; verified nonmatching
collisions, an in-flight save/reload, and immediate contacts across all 12 tiers.
