CAR JAM 2.0 — 3D TOWN — OCTOBER 17, 2026

A new visual presentation based on your supplied Car Jam reference.

WHAT CHANGED
- Actual 3D vehicles with rounded bodies, windows, tires, lights and large white direction arrows.
- Angled view of the lot, with five painted pickup spaces, animated waiting passengers, storefronts, striped awnings, trees and streetlights.
- Cars remain the same apparent size in the lot and the pickup spaces.
- Vehicles drive out toward the pickup area; passengers walk over to board; full vehicles depart.
- Landscape places the pickup spaces beside the lot and moves scenery away from playable areas.
- Taps select the 3D vehicle, with a forgiving footprint fallback.

PRESERVED
The existing passenger matching, two/four/six-seat vehicles, rare eight-seat bus, generated lots, look-ahead Hint, Undo and saved progress. No ads, locked paid bays or monetization added. Other games are unchanged.

INSTALL
Extract into your main Goobs-Games folder, preserving the included games/ folders and replacing the included files. Replace sw.js in the main directory. The 3D renderer and its offline dependencies are included. How to shows “Car Jam 2.0 · 3D town” so you can identify the new build.
