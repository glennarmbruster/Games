Crowd Cascade 2.0 — Bridge squad update

Upload these six files preserving folder paths. Accept the collection's update
prompt and reload after uploading. No other game files need to be replaced.

Changes
- Stone bridge over blue water, viewed from behind the squad.
- Dense blue runners and red enemy formations, with animated legs and weapons.
- Up to 160 visible runners; camera follows the squad and adjusts for its size.
- Continuous volleys that visibly travel to their targets before doing damage.
- Shoot blue recruit gates to increase their reward, or choose purple multipliers.
- Four weapon power levels from breakable gold crates.
- A large final enemy and a seven-second gold-truck bonus round replace stairs.
- Existing Crowd Cascade level and stars remain saved under the same key.
- Existing controls and the single combined Crowd Cascade game are retained.

How to play
Drag to steer and aim. Shooting is automatic. Choose gates to recruit runners,
shoot gold crates for stronger weapons, and clear or avoid red squads. Keep at
least one runner alive through the course. Aim at the gold truck in the bonus
round to earn extra stars. Later courses increase enemy strength.
