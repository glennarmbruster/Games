REBUS — VERSION 1.0.0

Upload the ZIP contents to the Goobs Games repository root, preserving folders.
Rebus appears in the Puzzles tab. The game and icon work offline after the
collection update is installed. No existing game files are changed.

HOW TO PLAY
Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard. Look at the size, color, position, direction,
and arrangement of the words. Type the familiar word or phrase and press Check.
Punctuation and small articles (a, an, the) do not matter.

There are 18 puzzles in each difficulty, 54 total. Use the arrows to review
clues; Next moves to another unsolved puzzle. Hint gives a nudge, then the
first word. Reveal shows the answer without marking that clue solved.
Progress is saved on this device in localStorage key goobs.rebus.v1.
