FRUIT FUSION + JUICE SLIDE — CONTINUE WHEN FULL
Fruit Fusion v1.7.0 / Juice Slide v1.6.0

Upload the four included files to matching paths in your existing collection.

When the bag or table fills, the game pauses and asks whether to Continue.
Continue removes the smallest pieces first: 30% rounded up, with a minimum of
three when available. At least the largest remaining piece is kept if there
was more than one. A single blocked piece can be cleared to recover the run.
Scores, best scores, next pieces and unlocked drinks are retained.
No limit or payment applies to continues. New game and Exit are also available.

The remaining fruit gets time to settle. A full-board prompt cannot immediately
repeat before the player has made another move. An escaped fruit that survives
the clearing is brought back into the bag.
Exit saves the continued run, including its recovery state.
Previously saved full runs also show the new Continue prompt when opened.

Keeps Juice Slide's straight launches and teak platform; keeps all current
fruit/drink artwork and sizes. Other games are unchanged.

Validation: actual fullness conditions, smallest-first removal, score/best/next
preservation, reload in another orientation, resumed play, empty and single-piece
cases, New game reset, and both normal Exit and pause-menu Exit save/resume.
Physical-device Safari testing was not available in this workspace.
