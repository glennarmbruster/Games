TRAFFIC JAM 1.0 — October 17, 2026

Upload the contents of this ZIP into the main Goobs Games directory, preserving
all folder paths. Replace index.html and sw.js when prompted. The new game is
under Puzzles. Accept the collection's Update available prompt after syncing.
Open the game while online once before using it offline.

A separate road-clearing puzzle inspired by the supplied screenshots.
Tap a car to follow its arrow at the next intersection, then continue straight
out of town. Straight, left, right and U-turn arrows are included. The whole
route must be clear before a car moves. A blocked car stays put and its blocker
flashes coral. Clear all cars to advance.

- Unlimited deterministic levels; layouts become denser and add more streets.
- Guaranteed solution by construction, with free Hint and Undo.
- No timer, lives, ads, purchases or locked power-ups.
- Colorful top-down cars, dark asphalt, landscaped town blocks, smooth turns.
- Integrated Restart / How to / Exit controls and Sound toggle.
- Portrait and landscape layouts; level and remaining cars saved locally.
- Dedicated Traffic Jam icon, Puzzles menu entry and offline cache group.

Car Jam and the other games' gameplay are unchanged. Shared arcade core/style
files are included as unchanged dependencies. No rollback ZIP included.

Validation: native Canvas/JavaScript runtime checks solved the first 60 levels
(1,326 cars). Five phone/tablet viewport sizes checked for tap transforms,
blocked routes, hints, animation, undo, completion and next-level navigation.
Save/restore and offline asset paths checked. This environment has no browser
runtime; actual Safari/iPhone/iPad behavior and visual layout need device testing.
