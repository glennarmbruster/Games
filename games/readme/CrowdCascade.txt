Crowd Cascade — two modes
Crowd Run: grow a running crowd through paired gates, avoid costly enemy clashes, collect coins and use jump pads.
Squad Assault: the former Squad Sprint, now a mode here. Aim the moving squad toward crates and enemies to fire automatically; collect recruits.
Both use a moving 3D course, running characters and a stair finish. Each saves its own level; old Squad Sprint level imports into Squad Assault. Drag to steer.
