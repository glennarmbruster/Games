CAR JAM 2.4
Replaced the distorted textured-mesh display with a crisp illustrated renderer
using the same scene coordinates, hit testing and vehicle movements. Cars now
use the detailed overhead artwork directly. Added original red, cyan, yellow,
green and violet minibus art, soft contact shadows, more readable passengers,
textured asphalt, paved sidewalk and detailed neighborhood illustrations.
The camera fits the active lot more tightly and uses a higher viewing angle.
Cars keep the same scale in the pickup bays and the puzzle. The waiting count
stays in the header. Puzzle generation, passenger order, vehicle capacities,
boarding, Undo and concurrent dispatch rules are unchanged.

TRAFFIC JAM 1.3
New detailed residential, shopping, market and park illustrations replace the
simple buildings and trees. Added textured asphalt, curb highlights, worn road
paint, entrance crossings, metal drains and stronger vehicle shadows. Scenery
continues to the screen edges. Uniform scaling keeps cars from being stretched.
Illustrations are cropped proportionally rather than distorted to fit blocks.
Road layout, turns, collision clearance, saved level and simultaneous movement
are unchanged.

for Classic, the clear-all-cars mode from the reference screenshots.
