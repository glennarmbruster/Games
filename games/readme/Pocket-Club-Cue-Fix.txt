POCKET CLUB 1.0.5 — CUE PULL FIX

This ZIP can be uploaded even if the Contraption Lab update has not been
applied yet. It includes both the Contraption Lab update and this cue fix.

Touch the visible pool cue and pull it backward. It follows your finger
without flipping the aim. Release after a pull to shoot. A brief tap does
not shoot. You can still aim by tapping or dragging on the felt, adjust with
the fine aim buttons, and use the Power slider plus Shoot button.

The larger numbered balls from Pocket Club 1.0.4 are preserved.
