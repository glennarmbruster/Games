How to play

  1. Hold ◀ or ▶ to slide your cannon. Tap FIRE to shoot. Only one of your shots can be on the screen at a time, so make each one count.
  2. The aliens march side to side and step down at each edge. They speed up as you shoot them down.
  3. Points: bottom rows 10, middle rows 20, top row 30. The saucer that crosses the top is worth 50 to 300.
  4. Hide under the four shields. Shots from both sides chew them away.
  5. You have 3 cannons and get one more at 1,500 points. If the aliens reach the ground, the game is over.
  6. Clear a wave and the next one starts lower.
