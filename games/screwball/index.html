How to play

Furnished house: tap House at the top to dismantle a complete loft home. Stairs, lamps, cushions, paintings, books and kitchen fittings each have real screws. Watch the next three box colors above the tray.

  1. Tap a screw to take it out. It flies into the box of its color at the top.
  2. Only screws with nothing in the way straight out from the head come out. If a piece covers a screw, take that piece off first.
  3. A piece falls off when its last screw is out. The level is done when every piece is off.
  4. Each box holds 3 screws. When it’s full, the next box comes in.
  5. No box for that color yet? The screw waits in the tray (3 spots) and hops into its box when one shows up.
  6. A 4th screw with no box ends the level — or watch a quick ad to get another tray spot and keep going. + Box (also a quick ad) adds a 4th or 5th box.
  7. Drag to turn it any way you like: all the way around, over the top and underneath, to find screws on every side. Pinch with two fingers to zoom in. Peek through windows: there's furniture inside.
