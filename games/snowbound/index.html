How to play

  • Launch & Glide: drag down on the mountain and release, or set power and tap Launch. Upgrade your sled after runs; Rocket level 3 installs dual thrusters.
  • Endless Ride: steer around trees and rocks. You have three chances.
  • Drag left or right to steer. Tap Jump to clear obstacles. Boost fires the rocket; ramps launch you into the air.
  • Collect stars for a powerup: gold refills boost, blue gives a shield, pink gives a speed surge.
  • Keyboard: ← → or A/D to steer, Space to jump, B for boost. Pause is at the top.
