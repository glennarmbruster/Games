How to play

  • Launch & Glide: set power, launch, then drag left or right to steer. Your sled slows gradually. Choose one upgrade after each run.
  • Endless Ride: steer to dodge trees and rocks. You have three chances. Speed rises as you travel.
  • Tap Jump to clear low obstacles. Ramps launch you automatically; glowing snowflakes give a short boost.
  • On a keyboard, use ← → or A/D to steer and Space to jump. Pause is always at the top.
