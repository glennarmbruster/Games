How to play

  1. Hold your phone sideways. The machine pitches. Flick your finger up through the ball as it reaches the dotted line, like swinging a bat.
  2. Timing aims it: early pulls it to left field, late pushes it to right. Flick faster to hit harder. A steep flick lifts it higher; a flatter one hits a line drive. (From behind the plate, a flick just under the ball lifts it too.)
  3. Every ball that goes 100 ft or more scores its distance in points; a home run scores double. Balloons are 50 more.
  4. Balls with faces do tricks: some curve, drop, stop in mid-air, wobble, or come three at once (only one is real; the fakes fade just before the line). Heavy grey balls don’t go far. A gold ball multiplies the distance.
  5. Minor and Major: each step has more balls. A miss, a foul or a short hit costs one bar of health; lose all five and the step is over.
  6. Moon-Star: 9 balls for a record. Hit the moon or the stars for big bonuses. Training: pick a pitch and practice.
  7. The camera button switches between the side view and the view from behind the plate. The sunglasses show the next pitches (a few uses per step). Points earn experience; each level up gives an upgrade: Power, Contact (a bigger sweet spot) or Batting Eye (more sunglasses).
