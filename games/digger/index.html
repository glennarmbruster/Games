How to play

  1. Use the joystick to dig through the dirt. You can only turn where the tunnels line up, like the arcade.
  2. Tap PUMP to throw your air pump at a puffer or a scorcher, then tap it again (or hold it) to pump. Four pumps and it pops. Stop pumping and it shrinks back.
  3. Dig under a rock and get out of the way: it wobbles, then drops and flattens anything below, you too. Crush several at once for big points.
  4. Deeper layers score more: 200, 300, 400, 500. A scorcher popped from the side is worth double. Watch out for its fire, which goes right through dirt.
  5. Enemies can float through the dirt as eyes to come after you. Drop two rocks and a bonus item shows up in the middle.
  6. The last enemy runs for the surface. Extra lives at 10,000 and 40,000, then every 40,000.
