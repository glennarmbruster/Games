How to play

  1. Put one animal in every row, every column and every color.
  2. Animals need elbow room: no two can touch, not even corner to corner.
  3. Tap a square to mark it × (no animal here). Tap again to clear it.
  4. Double-tap to put an animal there. Double-tap it again to take it away.
  5. Drag your finger across squares to mark lots of × at once.
  6. With hearts on (3 per puzzle), a wrong animal costs a heart and leaves a red × so you remember. Out of hearts? Watch a quick ad to keep going.
  7. Some levels are Tricky, and some Very tricky: they need a “try it and see” step (suppose an animal on a square and follow the rules until something runs out of room). Some have a color that's just one square — that animal is easy to spot. Every 20–30 levels you get a free animal already in place.
  8. Finish a level and the next one is waiting. Each level has its own animal: squirrels, dogs, cats and penguins take turns. Level numbers are the same puzzle on every phone.
  9. Daily (top right) is a new 11×11 every day with a surprise animal, the same for everyone.
  10. Stuck? Hint explains the next step and can do it for you.
