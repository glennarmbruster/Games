How to play

  1. The picture is cut into squares and mixed up.
  2. Drag a square onto another spot and they swap. Or tap a square, then tap where it goes.
  3. Squares that sit next to their right neighbor click together and move as one piece.
  4. A square that lands in its right spot flashes green. Turn on Lock pieces in place in Settings if you want those squares to stay put.
  5. Stuck? Peek shows the whole picture while you hold it. Hint puts one piece home: 3 per puzzle, then each costs one quick ad.
  6. Rotation challenge: enable it before a new puzzle. Select a single tile and use Rotate selected. Upright neighbors join; all tiles must be upright to finish.
  7. My photos: pick a picture from your phone and make a puzzle out of it.
