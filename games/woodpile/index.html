How to play

  1. Drag a piece from the bottom onto the board. Pieces can’t be turned.
  2. Fill a whole row, column or 3×3 square and it clears away.
  3. Use all three pieces to get three new ones.
  4. Clear several at once for a combo. Clear on move after move for a streak. Both score big.
  5. The game ends when none of your pieces fit.
  6. Undo: 3 per game. After that, each extra undo costs one quick ad.
  7. Classic goes for a high score. Daily gives everyone the same pieces today. Levels have a goal, like clearing squares or collecting gems.
