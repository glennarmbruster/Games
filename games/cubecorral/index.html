How to play

Sculpture gallery: six detailed scenes are available from Gallery at the top. Each lane shows its color and currently exposed cubes; the next three blasters show their shot counts. Exposed counts change as cubes are removed, so compare them with the blaster’s ammo before committing a slot.

  1. Tap the front blaster in any of the 3 lines. It moves up into a slot.
  2. Blasters shoot cubes of their own color that are showing: if you can see a cube from any side, it can be hit. One shot knocks off one cube.
  3. The number is how many shots it has left. When it runs out, it leaves and the slot opens up.
  4. If none of its color can be hit, it waits in its slot (you’ll see zzz). Knock off the cubes covering its color to uncover more.
  5. When all 5 slots are full and nobody can shoot, you’re jammed. Watch a quick ad for an extra slot, or try again.
  6. Drag the sculpture to turn it all the way around. Watch the numbers: a blaster with more shots than its color has showing will stop and wait. Bigger sculptures have surprise colors inside.
