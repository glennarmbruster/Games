How to play

  1. Put your finger on the lane, slide left or right to line up, then flick up. The ball rolls down the lane, hops off the ramp and lands in the rings.
  2. Flick speed is distance. Too soft and it drops short; too hard and it hits the back net. The direction of your flick aims it.
  3. A curved swipe puts a little spin on the ball so it curls on the lane.
  4. Rings score 10 to 50 from the outside in. The two small corner pockets are worth 100.
  5. A ball that rolls back to you is free: roll it again.
  6. Classic: 9 balls, go for your best score.
  7. Survival: beat the round’s target with 9 balls to move on to a new machine. One spot flashes each ball — land there for 5×.
  8. Tilt (optional, in Settings): tilt the phone to nudge the ball while it’s flying.
